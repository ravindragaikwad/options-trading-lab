Adding new trades

  1. On robinhood.com go to Account → Reports and statements → Reports, and create an account activity report (CSV) for the dates you want.
  2. Click Import Robinhood CSV here, or drop the file anywhere on this page.

Overlapping date ranges are fine. Rows already saved are skipped, so you can export the last week every Friday.

How P&L is counted

Each closing sale is matched to your earliest open contracts of the same option (first in, first out). The profit lands on the day you closed, and Robinhood's fees are included. Expired options close at $0. A partial exit counts on its own day. If a position was opened before your earliest import, its sale is skipped.
